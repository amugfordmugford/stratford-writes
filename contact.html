Common questions

Can I just come to a meeting?

Yes. There's no registration and no fee. Check the calendar for the next session and turn up. If you'd rather have a name to look for when you arrive, email us first and we'll tell you who to find.

Do I have to bring writing?

No. Plenty of people come to a first meeting just to listen. When you do want feedback, work is normally circulated about a week ahead so everyone can read it properly.

Can we mention your group in a listing or article?

Please do — email us and we'll send you a current description and the right contact details.
