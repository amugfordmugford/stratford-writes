About Us

Stratford Writes is a community group for people who write — whatever they write, and however far along they are.

  • Where Stratford, Ontario. Meeting locations are listed on each calendar entry.
  • When See the calendar for the current schedule.
  • Cost Free to attend.
  • Who can join Adults at any stage of writing. No application, no portfolio, no audition.

Our regular meetings

We currently have three types of regular meetings:

Weekly Write In | Coffee Culture | Sundays 3–5pm

Show up, find a seat, write and socialize.

Monthly Social Time

Once a month we meet at a different location to just hang out and chat. Specific details in the calendar.

Monthly Meeting | Every 3rd Thursday | The Bunker 7–9:30pm

Every month has its own theme. Sometimes we discuss specific topics, sometimes we share and offer friendly critiques of each others writing. But don't worry. It's voluntary to share (or not).

How a meeting works

Meetings run about two hours and follow a loose but reliable rhythm:

  1. Arrive and socialize 7–7:40pm.
  2. Workshop (about 90 minutes). We discuss member work that was circulated in advance. The writer listens first, then asks questions at the end or we discuss a specific topic like world building or marketing.

What we write

Literary and genre fiction, poetry, memoir and personal essay, plays, and the occasional stubbornly unclassifiable thing. Members have published in journals and anthologies, self-published, entered contests, and written purely for themselves. All of those are fine reasons to be in the room.

Ground rules

  • What's shared in the group stays in the group unless the writer says otherwise.
  • Come as you are and let others do the same — no put-downs of a person, a genre, or a level of experience.
  • Time is shared evenly. Everyone who brought work gets their turn.

Curious? Have a look at what's coming up, or send us a message and we'll answer your questions.
