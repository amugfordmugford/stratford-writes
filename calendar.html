Calendar

Meetings, workshops, and readings. Everything on this calendar is open to members and to anyone thinking about joining.

Subscribe to the calendar

Rather than checking back here, you can add our schedule to your own calendar app so new meetings appear automatically.

  • Google Calendar: use the + Google Calendar button in the bottom corner of the embed above.
  • Apple Calendar or Outlook: subscribe to the calendar's public iCal address — email us and we'll send you the link.

Coming to your first meeting?

No need to register — just show up. If you'd like a heads-up about what we'll be discussing, send us a note beforehand and we'll fill you in.
